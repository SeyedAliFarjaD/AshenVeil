## Description

The ash has been falling for an age. Every bonfire is cold, every hall is held by the dead, and at the end of the road an Ashen King sits on a throne of embers.

You are the last knight keeping the vigil.

**Ashen Vigil** is a 2D souls-like metroidvania that blends the fluid, airborne combat of *Hollow Knight* with the razor-edge deflects of *Nine Sols* and the bonfire-and-embers rhythm of *Dark Souls*.

### Fight with precision
- **Deflect** a blow at the last instant to stagger your foe, then answer with a crushing **riposte**.
- **Pogo** off enemies and hazards with a downward plunge.
- Build **focus** with every hit, then spend it on a **Soul Bolt** or unleash the **Force**, a burning finishing strike.
- Roll through danger, chain three-hit combos, and read every telegraph.

### Explore eight hand-built lands
1. **The Ashen Ramparts**: Ser Caedric, the Ashen Warden
2. **The Drowned Crypt**: Morwen, the Drowned Saint
3. **The Burning Cathedral**: the Twin Pyres, fought together
4. **The Frozen Belfry**: Sir Hallowyn, the Rimebound
5. **The Sunken Foundry**: Gorrak, the Forge Golem
6. **The Hollow Wood**: Oakhart, the Stag Spirit
7. **The Glass Citadel**: Seraphine, the Duelist Queen
8. **The Ashen Throne**: Vaelor, the Ashen King, in three stages

Each land grants a new ability (double jump, wall jump, air dash, grapple, glide) that opens the path ahead. Lava, saw blades, crumbling stones, ice, updrafts and moving platforms stand between you and every throne room, and false walls hide relics and ember caches for those who look closely.

### Rest, grow, return
- Spend embers at bonfires on **Vigor, Endurance, Strength and Attunement**.
- Collect and equip **charms** to shape your build.
- Beat the King and **New Game+** begins: the dead endure more, hit harder and are worth more.

### Built for players
- **Story, Normal and Hard** difficulty.
- Full **key rebinding** and **gamepad** support.
- Accessibility options: screen shake, bright flashes and damage numbers can be turned down or off.
- Pixel-perfect or fit-to-window scaling, fullscreen, three save slots, autosave.

Every sprite, tile, effect and most of the music in Ashen Vigil is generated in code.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Move | A / D or arrows | Stick / D-pad |
| Jump (again in mid-air to double jump) | Space / Z | A |
| Attack | J / X / left click | X / RB |
| Plunge (pogo) | Down + Attack in mid-air | Down + X |
| Roll / air dash | Shift / K / C | B |
| Parry | L | LT |
| Heal | F / Q | Y |
| Soul Bolt / Force | I / U | - |
| Rest at bonfire | E or W | LB |
| Grapple | R | RT |
| Pause | Esc | Start |

All keys can be rebound in **Settings → Controls**.
